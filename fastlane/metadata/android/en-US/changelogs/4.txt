0.4.0 (alpha)
- Linux apps can run adb on this phone and on USB devices (opt-in per app).
- Shared folders are writable and bring what's mounted inside them.
- Predictive back: the window and its pages shrink as you swipe.
- Graphics and common app libraries (libGL, Mesa, GTK, NSS) come with Linux.
- Recents names each Linux window on every launcher.
- Flathub apps uninstall from the home screen.
- Fixes: PC trackpad crash, Light/Dark switch, system files reopening.
