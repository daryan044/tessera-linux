0.2.0 (alpha)
- A Linux desktop: movable windows in your phone's colors, a dock and Start menu, icons and files on the desktop, full screen. Flathub apps open in it too.
- Search is a pill in the Apps bar, with full-screen results.
- Flathub works on a fresh system (CA certificates).
- Android 10 and older: sound and USB fixes.
- Chroot mode no longer leaves background processes running.
